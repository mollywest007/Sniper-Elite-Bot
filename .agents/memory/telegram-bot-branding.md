description: User requires the web app and Telegram bot screens to remain free of logos and promotional banner imagery, including deposit flows.
---

Do not display brand logos or promotional banner images in the web app or Telegram bot. This includes welcome/menu screens and deposit instructions. Keep functional interface icons that communicate actions or status; do not use them as brand marks.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that the logo be removed everywhere, including the deposit flow.

**How to apply:**
- Keep global web navigation free of brand marks, not just the dashboard.
- Telegram screens should be text-only; screen transitions from legacy photo messages should replace and remove the old photo when possible.
- Preserve functional menu buttons, deposit instructions, and action icons.
